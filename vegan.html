Gluten free and allergens

Our standard dough contains gluten. Gluten-free bases are available in 12", but we use wheat flour in our kitchens, so please talk to us before ordering if you have coeliac disease.

Our dough and all our sauces are free from all three, except the white base, which is a parmesan emulsion. Cheese and toppings can contain them, so check each pizza.

For food allergies and intolerances, please inform our staff before ordering.
