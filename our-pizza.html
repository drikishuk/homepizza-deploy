Our pizza

Think our pizzas taste different? That's because they are.

Every detail, from our dough to our toppings, has been meticulously crafted using pioneering techniques. Keep watching to uncover the secrets behind what makes our pizzas truly unique.

The secret behind our dough

96+ hours. 13 strains. One very well looked-after dough.

We push our dough through a 96+ hour process. That's over four days of slow, controlled fermentation, in a system that exists nowhere else on Earth.

Hour 0

You can't just write this down.

People ask us for the recipe all the time. But our dough isn't just made; it's raised like a favourite child, carefully nurtured and shaped over time.

Hour 6

Not just fermented. Powerful.

An ultra high protein flour blend. No tricks or protein powders, just wheat grown in locations with extreme weather conditions, giving it a rigid protein structure.

Hour 20

Thirteen strains. Bred, not found.

We didn't just find the right natural cultures, we built them: an entire ecosystem of wild yeasts and beneficial bacteria.

Yes, one was evolved from a sourdough starter. That doesn't mean our pizza is sourdough. It's that and so much more!

Hour 40

They evolve together.

Our cultures don't just coexist; they evolve together, adapting, mutating, and making our dough better with every single batch.

Hour 58

Cold and slow.

At cold temperatures, fermentation works slowly, unlocking layers of complexity you simply can't get any other way.

Hour 76

Lighter and sweeter.

Our cultures break down heavy starches, unlocking natural sweetness and creating a lighter texture.

And there are no unnecessary additives.

Hour 96

Exists nowhere else on Earth.

So no, we can't give you the recipe. It's not just a recipe, it's a living, breathing masterpiece, constantly evolving and shaped by time.

It takes space, time & care.

Four day fermentation isn't easy. But for us? It's the only way. And that's why nothing else tastes, or feels, like this.

Stretch

Stretched by hand.

When the dough is ready, we stretch each base by hand, pressing out from the middle and leaving a soft, puffy rim.

Tomato sauce

The finest sauce money can buy

Most places treat tomato sauce as an afterthought. We don't. We use Mutti, one of the finest tomato products in the world, so premium that most restaurants wouldn't even consider it.

Why it matters

No artificial thickeners, no funky preservatives: just perfectly ripe, sun grown tomatoes and a secret blend of natural herbs.

Cheese

100% mozzarella.

Then we scatter it over the sauce by hand. Most of our vegan pizzas use a vegan mozzarella, made from coconut oil and potato starch.

Pepperoni

Nitrate free pepperoni

Our pepperoni is salt-cured and completely nitrate free.

Why it matters

Traditional cured meats are usually made with nitrates. Ours has the same bold, rich flavour without them.

Chicken

Chicken like you've never tasted before

Forget dry, stringy chicken or processed cubes. Ours goes through a 14 hour sous vide process in a secret marinade, using only the finest premium breast meat.

Why it matters

Melt-in-your-mouth tenderness with flavour locked deep into every bite. The kind of chicken you'd expect in a high-end restaurant, not on a pizza.

Beef & lamb

Slow cooked meats, maximum flavour

Premium lean beef topside and fresh whole New Zealand lamb legs, both slow-cooked for 14 hours, breaking down tough fibres into something so tender it practically melts.

Why it matters

The rich, natural broth from cooking becomes a sauce that keeps the meat juicy and exploding with umami. Every bite is as good as the first.

Vegetables

Fresh, hand cut veg

No sad, pre packaged toppings here. Every vegetable is hand cut and roasted in house.

Why it matters

Roasting locks in natural sweetness and depth of flavour. It's a small detail, but one that makes a huge difference.

A culinary experience like no other

Time to pick your perfect pizza.

We approach pizza with the same precision and creativity you'd expect from a Michelin starred kitchen. It's not just pizza, it's a labour of love, science, and artistry.

How we make it

  1. 1. 96+ hours of dough
