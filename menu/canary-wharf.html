Good to know

  • All our pizzas have basil-infused tomato base & 100% mozzarella unless stated otherwise.
  • Size is 13", cut into 8 slices. Served crispy & well done.
  • Dough and all our sauces are dairy, egg & soya free. White base is a unique parmesan emulsion.
  • Gluten-free bases available in 12", and all of our toppings and sauces are gluten free.
  • Vegan mozzarella is made from coconut oil and potato starch.
  • Some menu items contain nuts. Our meats may contain small bones.
  • Prices include VAT at the current rate and are subject to change without notice.
  • For food allergies and intolerances, please inform our staff before ordering. Full allergen chart (PDF)
