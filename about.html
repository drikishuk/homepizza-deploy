About us

We wanted to question the usual approach to pizza making and see how far we could take it.

While respecting the craft and the traditions that have shaped pizza, we also believe that there is room to experiment.

It was vital to rethink each element, from fermentation and flavour to structure and cooking techniques.

  • Fermentation
  • Flavour
  • Structure
  • Cooking techniques

The result is a style of pizza that is distinctively our own, built around carefully sourced ingredients and a constant desire to improve.

For us, great pizza is about more than just following a recipe. It is about creating something that tastes exceptional and leaves our customers feeling incredible.

Our story

25 years of family passion

Our family's 25 years of passion and knowledge is lovingly constructed into every pizza.

  1. Once upon a slice

    South Africa

    Our parents crafted pizza magic in 5 bustling shops.

    The roots still show on the menu: the Biltong pizza, with thinly sliced South African cured beef.

  2. But adventure called

    To the UK

    And they journeyed to the UK.

  3. The first UK kitchen

    Setting up in Gerrards Cross: still our first, best and most popular location.

  4. Fast forward 15 years

    The pizza loving kids have joined the party!
