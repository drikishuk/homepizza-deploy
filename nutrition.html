Nutritional info

You can eat pizza on a diet, as long as it's @homepizza.

As our pizzas come with a wide variety of sauces, cheeses and toppings, we've tested just our signature dough. Stated serving is for a whole pizza base.

For reference

The Margherita benchmark

Margherita pizzas contain more cheese than those with toppings. For topping pizzas, this substitution of some cheese for toppings tends to balance the overall calorie count. However, pizzas with extra cheeses or richer toppings will naturally be higher in calories.

  • 12" Margherita770kcal, whole pizza
  • 14" Margherita1080kcal, whole pizza
  • The diet-friendly one~500kcal, your way If you're looking for a diet friendly, 500 calorie pizza, just ask for little or no cheese and add some delicious vegetables and/or proteins.
