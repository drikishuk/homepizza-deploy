Nutrition and allergens

What's in our pizza

Allergens first, then calories and what goes into our dough.

If you have an allergy

For food allergies and intolerances, please inform our staff before ordering.

See every pizza's allergensAllergen chart (PDF)Vegan and gluten free
Nuts
Some menu items contain nuts.
Small bones
Our meats may contain small bones.
Gluten
Our standard dough contains gluten. Gluten-free bases are available in 12", but we use wheat flour in our kitchens, so please talk to us before ordering if you have coeliac disease.
Dairy, egg and soya
Our dough and all our sauces are free from all three, except the white base, which is a parmesan emulsion. Cheese and toppings can contain them, so check each pizza.

Calories

Our Margherita

Margherita pizzas contain more cheese than those with toppings. For topping pizzas, this substitution of some cheese for toppings tends to balance the overall calorie count. However, pizzas with extra cheeses or richer toppings will naturally be higher in calories.

  • 12" Margherita770kcal, whole pizza
  • 14" Margherita1080kcal, whole pizza
  • A lighter pizza~500kcal, your way For a pizza of about 500 kcal, ask for little or no cheese and add vegetables or protein.
