Gluten free and allergens

Gluten-free bases at every pizzeria.

Our standard dough contains gluten. Gluten-free bases are available in 12", but we use wheat flour in our kitchens, so please talk to us before ordering if you have coeliac disease. For food allergies and intolerances, please inform our staff before ordering.
